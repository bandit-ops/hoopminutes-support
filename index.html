HOOP MINUTES

Here to help, coach.

Hoop Minutes tracks basketball playing time, rotations and scoring margins. Your records stay on your device, with no app account required.

How do I back up my records?

Save your current game first. In Settings, open Backups & device storage and choose Save backup file. Keep that file somewhere outside the app, such as your chosen file storage or another device.

A CSV export is a report, not a restorable backup. Use a backup file if you want to bring your teams, players, seasons and saved games back into Hoop Minutes.

How do I restore or transfer records?

On the destination device, open Settings and choose Restore from backup file. Select your Hoop Minutes backup and review the import before confirming. Imports add missing saved records. Existing records win conflicts, previously deleted records stay deleted, and the active game and settings are not replaced.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. Tracking and local records work offline. Internet may be needed for downloading or updating the app, emailing support, or accessing a backup you chose to store online.

What happens if I lose my phone or delete the app?

Records may be lost. We do not hold a server copy and cannot recover local records remotely. Keep backup files elsewhere. If the app reports a storage problem, avoid uninstalling it or erasing its data; contact support first.

How do I delete player information?

Deleting a player from the roster does not remove their details from saved games. Delete affected games too, or use Erase all local data in Settings to remove all app records on that device. Remove exported files and shared copies separately. See the privacy policy for details.

Is this the official game clock?

No. Hoop Minutes is a coaching aid. Playing time and scoring margins depend on the substitutions, clock controls and scores you record. Check important statistics against your own records.
